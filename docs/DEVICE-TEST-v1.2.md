# FinTrack v1.2 device test

Run on 28 Sep 2026 on a separate AVD, `fintest-split` (Android 14, x86_64, 1080×2340 @ 440 dpi), so the shared
`fintest` emulator and its ₹4,218 baseline were not touched. It used the debug build with the built-in OpenAI key, so
the AI judge made **real** OpenAI calls. The fixtures are generated by `gen_fixtures.py` (kept outside the repo):

| Input | Contents |
|---|---|
| SMS inbox (16) | Ronak's weekend, 19–22 Sep: SBOW ₹12,000 for 12, Uber ₹600 for 3, Rapido ₹180 alone, Rahul ₹1,000, Priya ₹1,200 (SBOW + Uber share in one transfer), Amit ₹200, 9 more friends ₹1,000, salary ₹85,000 |
| `hdfc_weekend.csv` | The same weekend as an HDFC CSV statement |
| `sbi_poptates.pdf` | 2-page text PDF, locked with password `01011990`: Pop Tates ₹12,340 for 12 on 5 Sep, 11 × ₹1,030 back, Rapido ₹150 |
| `icici_trip.xlsx` | Real Excel (date cells): 3 × ₹2,000 collected before an ₹8,000 MakeMyTrip booking (advance); Toit ₹1,800 paid back ₹500 + ₹700 (uneven shares) |
| `gpay_1.png`, `gpay_2.png` | Two overlapping Google Pay history screenshots: Uber ₹900 on 24 Sep, Karan and Sneha ₹300 each, Swiggy ₹349, Chai Point ₹60 |

Android 14 ignores `content insert` into the SMS inbox from the shell (even as root), so the inbox was seeded by
writing the telephony database directly as root (`seed_split.sql`).

## Bugs found on the device and fixed (each now has a unit test)

1. **The AI was asked about what the rules had already solved exactly, and answered worse.** It put all of Priya's ₹1,200
   on SBOW, so the app could only suggest. Now payments the local rules explain with high confidence are not sent to the AI.
2. **Loose rounding.** ₹8,000 ÷ 7 = ₹1,142.86 "rounded" to ₹1,200 let the hotel booking claim Priya's transfer. Rounded
   shares must now stay within 3% of the real share. One friend out of many expected is now weak evidence.
3. **Advance vs payback.** A payment explained both ways keeps the more convincing explanation.
4. **Weak local guesses vetoed confident AI answers.** A 30% guess no longer counts as disagreement.
5. **Far-off rivals counted as ambiguity.** A transfer 40 minutes after a cab is not ambiguous with a dinner 11 days earlier.
6. **₹ misread by OCR.** On-device OCR dropped the ₹ ("349"), read it as a letter ("T900"), or read it as a 7
   ("+ 7300" for ₹300, "760" for ₹60). The amount's box width now decides: amounts are right-aligned in one font, so a box three
   glyphs wide is "₹60".
7. **A quick local run undid AI-found splits** (after an undo, or on every incoming SMS). Only an AI run may now change them.
8. **Noise in the AI request.** Transfers from *before* a payment were sent too, and the model called them paybacks. The
   verifier rejected that, but the real split was lost. Only transfers after each payment are sent now, and the prompt spells
   out the order and the "less than the bill" rules. The verifier is what kept every wrong AI answer out of the numbers.
9. "Toit Brewpub" was treated as a person: pub, brewery, bar, lounge and similar words are now food and drink.

Also: a suggested split now says "your share would be…" instead of "counted as your spend", and friends' paybacks
have their own line on Home ("Paid back by friends").
