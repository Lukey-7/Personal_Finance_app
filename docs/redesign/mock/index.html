Quiet ledger

The direction for FinTrack's redesign, shown on three screens at real size (393dp wide). Warm page and hairlines stay; depth comes from raised and sunken surfaces, money gets its own face with lined-up figures, and one deep ink-blue marks everything you can tap. Figures are sample data.

What changes everywhere

  • Deep ink-blue (#1F3BD6, #9DA8FF at night) is the only tappable colour.
  • Four surface levels: page, card, raised (sheets, the Add button, urgent cards) and sunken (inputs, segment tracks, keypad).
  • Money in Inter Tight with lined-up figures; it never wraps or cuts.
  • Springy sheets, totals that roll to new values, a row that grows into its detail screen. All of it stops when Android's "Remove animations" is on.

What stays exactly as it is

  • Every number and how it is worked out; every figure still opens the payments behind it.
  • The same money format: ₹1,05,000, paise only when present, the minus before the ₹.
  • No network, no analytics, no new libraries. The new font ships inside the app.

New screens

  • Transaction detail: read first, edit second, with the SMS, splits, refunds and tax in one place.
  • Settings as a short index of eight pages instead of one long scroll.
  • Money tools as a grid of tiles, each showing its live number.
